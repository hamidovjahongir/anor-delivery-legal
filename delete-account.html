Anor Delivery – Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the Anor Delivery mobile app, published by Anorbank, can request deletion of their account and the associated data.

Who uses this app

Anor Delivery is a work app for Anorbank couriers and field agents. Accounts are not created by users themselves – they are issued by Anorbank to its employees and partners. For this reason, account deletion is handled by Anorbank on request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Contact Anorbank customer support using the official contact details published at anorbank.uz, or contact your Anorbank supervisor.
  2. State that you want to delete your Anor Delivery account.
  3. Provide the username or phone number linked to your Anor Delivery account so that we can verify your identity.
  4. After your identity is verified, your account will be deactivated and your data will be deleted as described below. You will be notified once the request is completed.

Data that is deleted

  • Account profile: name, username and phone number.
  • Login sessions and authentication tokens.
  • Device identifiers and device information (device ID, model, brand, OS version).
  • Push notification tokens.

Data that may be retained

Some records are part of Anorbank's banking operations and must be kept to comply with the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan. These records may be retained after your account is deleted, for the period required by law:

  • Records of client visits and their results, including photos and videos attached to them.
  • Call history with clients (phone number, date, time and duration).
  • Location data linked to completed visits.

Retained records are no longer linked to an active account and are accessible only to authorized Anorbank staff.
